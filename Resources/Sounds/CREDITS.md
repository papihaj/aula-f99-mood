# Typing sounds

All three sounds are recordings released under CC0 (public domain), so they
can be shipped with the app without attribution. The credits are here anyway.
Each sound uses 10 takes, converted to mono, trimmed to start at the keystroke
and limited to 0.3 seconds.

| Sound | Source | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| Tactile | "Keyboard_Tactile_1–10" from the [Mechanical Keyboards](https://freesound.org/people/StavSounds/packs/42151/) pack by StavSounds (freesound) | CC0 |
| Clicky | "Keyboard_Clicky_1–10" from the same pack | CC0 |
| Soft | keypress-001–010 from [Keyboard Soundpack #1](https://opengameart.org/content/keyboard-soundpack-1-typing-and-single-keystrokes) by unicaegames (OpenGameArt) | CC0 |
