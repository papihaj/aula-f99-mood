# Changelog

What's new in each version of F99 Mood.

## Unreleased

- New: a home side panel, white and dark themes, a 3D keyboard picture, and
  thocky and creamy typing sounds.
- Fixed: a scheduled mood now switches on even if your Mac was asleep at that
  time, or a clock change (such as daylight saving) skipped it.
- Better VoiceOver labels on the alert, colour and icon buttons.
- The README now lists every permission the app can ask for, and says the app
  never connects to the internet.
- Each release comes with a SHA-256 checksum, and the install command checks
  it and the app's signature before installing.

## 1.0 (13 October 2026)

The first release.

- Moods: pick a lighting style and switch with a click or a shortcut. The light
  strip follows the mood: a gradient, one steady colour, or off.
- Live effects drawn on your Mac and sent to the keys.
- Music: the keys move with the music playing on your Mac or in the room.
- Macros: shortcuts that type text or open an app. Macros never type into
  password fields.
- Extras: switch moods on a schedule; flash the keys for calendar events, new
  mail and new Messages or Slack messages; typing sounds.
- Reset all settings brings back the built-in moods.
- Runs on macOS 13 Ventura and later, on Apple silicon and Intel Macs.
