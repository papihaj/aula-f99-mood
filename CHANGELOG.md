# Changelog

What's new in each version of F99 Mood.

## 1.0 (21 October 2026)

The first release.

- Moods: pick a lighting style and switch with a click or a shortcut. The light
  strip follows the mood: a gradient, one steady colour, or off.
- Live effects drawn on your Mac and sent to the keys.
- Home: click keys or the light strip on the keyboard picture to paint them,
  in a 2D or 3D picture. Drag the bar under the picture to make it smaller.
- When F99 Mood is closed: pick a keyboard effect that stays on when you
  quit, unplug the keyboard or use it with another computer.
- Colorful Rainbow Vibes: soft rainbow waves, one of the built-in moods.
- Menu bar: dance to music, lights off and your three most-used moods.
- Music: the keys move with the music playing on your Mac or in the room.
- Macros: shortcuts that type text or open an app. Macros never type into
  password fields.
- Extras: switch moods on a schedule (even if your Mac was asleep at that
  time); flash the keys for new mail and new Messages or Slack messages;
  typing sounds, including thocky and creamy ones.
- Light and dark themes, with Mac-style buttons and menus.
- Reset all settings brings back the built-in moods.
- Runs on macOS 13 Ventura and later, on Apple silicon and Intel Macs.
- The app never connects to the internet. Each release comes with a SHA-256
  checksum, and the install command checks it and the app's signature before
  installing.
